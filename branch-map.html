Taming the divergences

Not sixty flows. One flow, swappable payloads.

The branching feels infinite because goal × sub-goal × level × skill is a big number. But the screens never change — only the content poured into them does. Fix the machine once; fill it flow by flow. This map is the machine, plus the first payload built end-to-end.

Fixed · built once

The structure

The same for every learner, every branch. Build it, then never rebuild it.

  • Screen types, order & transitions (the whole spine)
  • The activation loop shape: speak / read → score → what-to-improve → plan
  • The component kit: phone shell, progress bar, options, buttons, ack template
Varies · data, not screens

The payload

A content profile injected into the fixed screens. A "branch" is just a new payload.

  • The scenario, the prompt, the hint, the sample answer
  • Which activation exercise runs (from level × skill)
  • The 2–3 feedback focuses & which ack screens fire
01

The activation grid — level × skill

The skill (step 19) picks the exercise; the level (step 22, hybrid-confirmed) sets its format and difficulty. Twelve exercise templates cover everyone. The JTBD only supplies the scenario these run inside. speak · read

Skill ↓ / Level →BeginnerIntermediateAdvanced
Fluency Guided turn ●Answer a simple prompt with a sentence-starter scaffold. Impromptu turn ●Speak ~25s, hint available on tap. ★ golden path Open turn + follow-up ●No scaffold; Sarah asks a follow-up.
Pronunciation Read a line ○Short target line, syllable-level feedback. Read a sentence ○Tricky-sound sentence, phoneme feedback. Minimal pairs / stress ○Paragraph, stress & intonation.
Grammar Build a sentence ●Complete a spoken sentence from a frame. Respond & correct ●Speak a reply; grammar-focused feedback. Reformulate ●Fix / rephrase a flawed line aloud.
Vocabulary Name & use ●Use target words with a picture/prompt. Use in scenario ●Work target words into a spoken answer. Paraphrase / synonyms ●Swap in advanced vocabulary in context.

Read it as: Beginner leans read-aloud / guided (lower speaking load), Advanced leans open impromptu. The hybrid check in the activation can bump someone up or down a column after their first attempt.

Golden path · Goal A → interview · Intermediate × Fluency

The mock-interview activation

internal brief: [occupation] · [age] · "ace a job interview" · fluency → impromptu turn, interview scenario
Scenario
Sarah is the hiring manager. "You're in the interview now — I'll ask, you answer, just like the real thing." The room, not a lesson.
Prompt
Sarah asks"Tell me a little about yourself, and why you want this job."
Hint scaffold
Tap for a hint"I have ___ years of experience in ___. I'm good at ___. I want this job because ___."
Available but not pushed — using it is fine, and quietly flags a possible level bump-down.
Capture
~25s response. Real mic waveform + count-up ring. Sarah listens; no interruptions.
Feedback focuses
1
Smooth phrasing — pauses & restarts mid-sentence ("you paused 4 times — interviews reward steady phrasing").
2
Filler control — um / eh / "este" count ("6 fillers — we'll train interview-calm").
3
Complete answers — did the answer actually land the point ("strong content, let's tighten the ending").
Score reveal
Their own voice, scored on the three above (fluency-weighted). This is the real deficit reveal — frame it as a starting point, not a grade (Gleam pattern).
Ack screens (this branch)
Pay-raise stat (after occupation) Interview-nerves empathy (after JTBD) "The Silent Knower" reveal (after level) Interview-ready graph (pre-plan) Future-self letter (post-loader)
Plan framing
"[Name]'s Interview Plan — 3 weeks to interview-ready." Wk 1: smooth phrasing under pressure · Wk 2: the 10 questions you'll be asked · Wk 3: full mock interviews with Sarah. Mirrors the exact fixes from their scored turn.

How we actually build

Flow by flow, spine outward. Each step reuses everything before it.

1

This map → lock the golden-path payload

Agree the interview payload above (copy, focuses, plan). It's the spec everything downstream builds toward.

2

The spine screens (Acts 0 → 1 → 1.5 → identity)

Real HTML: phone shell, progress bar, transitions, option/chip/button kit, ack template. Branch-free — every payload inherits it.

3

The golden vertical

Career/interview all the way through: branching questions → derived brief → mock-interview activation → feedback → plan → paywall. One complete, demoable flow.

4

Fan out, one leaf at a time

Add the next sub-goal (e.g. "understand fast coworkers") as a new payload row against the same components. Repeat across the tree by traffic priority.
