The onboarding is the product demo nobody's bought yet.

The whole app is locked behind a hard paywall, so the flow has to manufacture full conviction before a single lesson. This is the canonical act-by-act sequence, synthesized from the eight flows we tore down and tuned to your calls: Bitepal's context-screen rhythm, me+'s mockup-led value open, and our own unfair advantage — the learner hears their own scored voice before they pay.

Where Sarah lives: she's the product's tutor, so she stars in the value-prop mockups and the hands-free calling demo, and she runs the activation. She is not the host of the Get-to-Know-You questions — those are clean, Gleam-style Q&A with no avatar narrating. Product tutor, yes; onboarding mascot, no.

Speak — the minimalist

Cross-checked against your two primary rivals

You asked whether the sequence holds up against what Speak and Praktika actually do. It's built to sit between them and beat both on the one thing that matters — conviction per minute.

vs Speak 9 screens · ~4 min

  • We adopt its best idea: spoken open answers as high-signal input (our Act 3).
  • We go further: Speak never scores the voice or shows a gap — we do, on their own words.
  • We add what it lacks: Speak shows "projection only, no plan reveal." Our Act 4 named plan is the conviction Speak skips.

vs Praktika 49 screens · ~15 min

  • We borrow its depth-with-context and the "50× cheaper than a tutor" anchor (Act 1).
  • We refuse its 15-minute slog: Bitepal's context/ack rhythm makes our depth feel like momentum, not interrogation.
  • We out-activate it: Praktika's aha is watching a synthetic face. Ours is hearing your own voice improve.
