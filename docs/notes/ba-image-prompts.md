# Before/After image prompts · 12 occupations x 2 = 24 images

## How to use
- Every prompt below is self-contained: copy one, paste into your image tool, generate.
- **Pair consistency is everything.** Generate the BEFORE first, then use it as a character/style reference (Midjourney `--cref`, or "use previous image as reference" in your tool) when generating the AFTER. Same person, same outfit, same room. Only posture, expression, lighting mood and energy change.
- Keep one fixed seed per pair if your tool supports it.
- Aspect: **1:1**. No text, no logos, no watermarks in the image.
- Naming convention when you export: `ba_office_before.png`, `ba_office_after.png`, `ba_business_before.png` … (matches the occupation ids in the prototype: office, business, jobseek, student, healthcare, restaurant, retail, construction, factory, driver, home, other). I will wire them in by these names.

## The system (baked into every prompt)
- **Style base:** flat modern vector illustration, warm cartoon proportions (same world as our Sarah avatar), waist-up, slight 3/4 camera, clean shapes, subtle grain, premium dark-mode app aesthetic. Deep dark indigo-navy background `#141127` with a soft radial glow behind the figure so it blends into our dark cards.
- **BEFORE state:** frozen mid-answer. Shoulders pulled in, gaze slightly down or away, one hand fidgeting or gripping something, tight mouth. Palette desaturated grey-lavender, dim cool lighting, the environment slightly faded around them.
- **AFTER state:** same person, mid-sentence and enjoying it. Upright open posture, eye contact toward the off-frame listener, one hand gesturing naturally, easy confident smile. Indigo `#6C63FF` rim light plus a warm golden accent glow, colors richer and cleaner.
