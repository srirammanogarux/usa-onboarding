# The plan hero — what changes with what

The hero must vary on four inputs: **goal · JTBD · timeframe · minutes a day.**
They do not all do the same job, and stacking all four into one sentence produces mush. Each one
gets a specific role.

| Input | Its job in the hero |
|---|---|
| **Timeframe** | Decides *whether the promise is about a conversation or about a life* |
| **JTBD** or **Goal** | Supplies the promise itself, whichever the timeframe selected |
| **Minutes × timeframe** | Decides *how bold the promise is allowed to be* |
| **Minutes** | Appears literally in the subline, and sets the effort framing |

---

## 1 · Timeframe decides the subject

Sriram's rule, and it is the right one: **a two-week promise can only credibly be about one specific
conversation. A six-month promise can credibly be about a life change.** Overclaim in the short lane
and nobody believes you; underclaim in the long lane and nobody pays.

| Timeframe | Days | Lane | Title is about | JTBD goes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 weeks | 14 | **Short** | their JTBD | the title |
| 1 month | 30 | **Short** | their JTBD | the title |
| 3 months | 90 | **Long** | their goal | the subline, then section 2 and week one |
| 6 months | 180 | **Long** | their goal | the subline, then section 2 and week one |
| 1 year | 365 | **Long** | their goal | the subline, then section 2 and week one |

**Boundary to confirm:** "less than a month" strictly means only *2 weeks*. I have put *1 month* in
the short lane too, because 30 days is still a single-conversation horizon. Say if you want it moved.

---

## 2 · Intensity decides how bold

`minutes × days` = total practice hours. Three tiers, and the promise verb changes with them, so a
5-minute two-week plan never makes a claim a 60-minute year plan should make.

|  | 5m | 10m | 15m | 30m | 40m | 60m |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **2 weeks** | light 1h | light 2h | light 3h | light 7h | std 9h | std 14h |
| **1 month** | light 2h | light 5h | std 7h | std 15h | std 20h | std 30h |
| **3 months** | std 7h | std 15h | std 22h | std 45h | deep 60h | deep 90h |
| **6 months** | std 15h | std 30h | std 45h | deep 90h | deep 120h | deep 180h |
| **1 year** | std 30h | deep 60h | deep 91h | deep 182h | deep 243h | deep 365h |

Light `< 7.5h` · Standard `7.5–45h` · Deep `> 45h`

Verb ladder:

| Tier | Shape of the claim |
|---|---|
| **light** | *stop* doing the bad thing — "stop freezing on the first question" |
| **standard** | *do* the good thing — "answer the first question without freezing" |
| **deep** | do it *without noticing* — "walk in without a single line rehearsed" |

## 3 · The title, assembled

```
   [TIME PHRASE], [NAME],
   [CLAIM — from JTBD if short, from goal if long, at the tier the commitment buys]
```

### Short lane · JTBD-led · 11 families × 3 tiers

| Family | light | standard | deep |
|---|---|---|---|
| interview | stop freezing on the first question | answer every question without freezing | walk in with nothing rehearsed |
| pitch | say your price out loud without apologising | say your price and hold it | name your price like it was never a question |
| crew | be understood the first time you say it | say the plan once and have it land | run the brief without repeating yourself |
| meetings | say one thing in the meeting | say the thing in the meeting, not after it | speak first, and have the room follow |
| fastspeech | stop asking people to repeat | follow fast speakers first time | stop noticing that they are fast |
| customer | take a call without rehearsing it | handle any customer call, calm and clear | take the call you used to pass on |
| services | book the appointment yourself | handle the doctor, the bank, the office alone | walk into any office and sort it out |
| smalltalk | say the first sentence | start the conversation instead of waiting | be the one people end up talking to |
| family | speak to her teacher without a script | speak to her teacher as an equal | be the parent who asks the hard question |
| exam | stop losing marks to hesitation | walk in with nothing left to guess | speak like the band you want |
| pronunciation | be asked to repeat less | be understood the first time | stop thinking about how you sound |

### Long lane · goal-led · 6 goals × 3 tiers

| Goal | light | standard | deep |
|---|---|---|---|
| career | English stops slowing you down at work | English stops holding your career back | English stops being why you are passed over |
| ielts | your band stops being a guess | your band becomes a decision, not a gamble | you walk in knowing the number |
| convo | conversations get easier to start | the conversations happen without planning them | you stop rehearsing before you speak |
| travel | you handle the basics on your own | you land anywhere and handle it yourself | you travel without thinking about English |
| school | you speak up in class more often | you speak in class before rehearsing it | your English stops capping your marks |
| other | English gets out of the way | English stops being the thing in the way | you stop noticing English at all |

**Total new copy: 51 lines** (33 + 18). If that is too many, the tier axis can collapse to two
(modest / full) for 34.

### The subline carries the rest

| Lane | Subline |
|---|---|
| Short | `B1 → B2 · 15 minutes a day` |
| Long | `Starting with defending your price · B1 → Advanced · 15 minutes a day` |

The long lane is where JTBD gets demoted to a supporting role — exactly as Sriram asked — and then
comes back at full size in section 2 (profile), the bento's conversation mock, and week one.

### Minutes also sets the effort framing

| Minutes | Phrase |
|---|---|
| 5, 10 | in the gaps of your day |
| 15, 30 | 15 minutes a day |
| 40, 60 | an hour a day |

The last one is the test case that matters: the smallest possible commitment still gets a true,
specific, dignified promise instead of an inflated one.

## 5 · The graph must follow the same lanes

Today it is a fixed curve with "Week 3" and a hardcoded 6 months.

| Varies with | What changes |
|---|---|
| level | start point of the curve |
| target | end point (level + 2, capped) |
| timeframe | **the x-axis units** — weeks for the short lane, months for the long lane |
| intensity | the steepness of the curve, so 60m/day visibly climbs faster than 5m/day |
| JTBD | the milestone label at the inflection, named per family rather than "Week 3" |

Short lane axis: `Day 1 · Week 1 · Week 2`
Long lane axis: `Now · Month 1 · Month 3 · October`
