# Speaking task content

Every scenario, question and model answer, one row per cohort. **This file
is generated** by `node tools/export-docs.js` for reading and review.
To change the copy, edit [`practice.js`](../practice.js) and rerun it.

## The 12 cohorts

### `career|office` · Working professional

> **"How are things going at work?"**

| step | the learner says |
|---|---|
| Say how it is going | Things are going well at work. |
| Give one detail | I am busy, but I like my team. |
| Say what is next | We finish a big project this week. |
| Close it | After that it should be calmer. |

Drilled afterwards: **project**, **finish**

### `career|ownboss` · Freelancer or business owner

> **"So what do you charge for this?"**

| step | the learner says |
|---|---|
| Say the price | My price for this is two thousand. |
| Say what is included | That covers the work and two changes. |
| Give the reason | It takes me about three weeks. |
| Hold your price | I think that is a fair price. |

Drilled afterwards: **thousand**, **covers**

### `career|jobhunt` · Looking for a job

> **"So, why should we hire you?"**

| step | the learner says |
|---|---|
| Answer it directly | I am a good fit for this position. |
| Give your reason | I stay calm when things get busy. |
| Give an example | Last month I ran our busiest week. |
| Close it | I know I can do the same here. |

Drilled afterwards: **position**, **busiest**

### `career|careerbreak` · On a career break

| Bring it back | I am ready to work full time now. |

### `career|other` · Something else

> **"Tell me about yourself."**

| step | the learner says |
|---|---|
| Start with now | Right now I work in customer support. |
| Say one strength | I am good at staying calm with people. |
| Give an example | Last year I handled our busiest month. |

Drilled afterwards: **customer**, **busiest**

### `convo` · Improve social conversations

> **"So what are you doing these days?"**

| step | the learner says |
|---|---|
| Answer it | I am good. Still at the same job. |
| Add one detail | I work with people every day. |
| Say what is new | I started running in the mornings. |
| Ask them back | What about you? |

Drilled afterwards: **people**, **mornings**

## Beginner affirmations (A1 and A2)

|---|---|---|
